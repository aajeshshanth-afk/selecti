SELECTICORE v69 — ELITE QUESTION QUALITY

This build strengthens the content layer rather than merely changing the UI.

Quality principles:
- Current NSW Selective structure is preserved.
- Maths and Thinking use multi-step, conditional, comparison, reverse and unfamiliar-application reasoning.
- Reading uses multiple source texts/genres, evidence, inference, author purpose, structure, tone, vocabulary and application questions.
- Writing prompts require controlled narrative development, foreshadowing, character motivation, purposeful dialogue and meaningful endings.
- Difficulty is Advanced / Challenge / Extreme Challenge; there is no Foundation tier in the elite generator.
- Every generated test passes structural and automated quality gates before being returned.
- Answer choices are checked for uniqueness and valid answer indexing.
- Explanations and skill/topic metadata are required.

Important limitation:
Automated generation and QA are not the same as human item-writer review. This package must not be represented as official NSW or James Ruse material. For commercial launch, a qualified educator/item-writing review pass is still recommended for the highest-stakes questions.
