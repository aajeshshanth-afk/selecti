SELECTICORE v69 — ELITE SELECTIVE CHALLENGE BANK

The 16,000 numbered tests use a harder Selective-style generation layer.

Design goals:
- NSW current structure: Reading 17 questions / 38 answers / 45 min;
  Mathematical Reasoning 35 / 40 min; Thinking Skills 40 / 40 min;
  Writing 1 / 30 min.
- Multi-step mathematical reasoning using Year-6 concepts.
- Conditional, ordering, set, sequence and deduction problems in Thinking Skills.
- Longer evidence-rich Reading passages with inference, comparison, tone,
  purpose, structure and evidence questions.
- Writing prompts requiring controlled narrative development, foreshadowing,
  purposeful dialogue, character motivation and meaningful endings.
- Close distractors and time-aware question design.

Important:
This is original SelectiCore practice content. It is not official NSW material,
not an official James Ruse test, and does not guarantee any school placement.
The NSW Department says the placement test measures ability using concepts from
the NSW curriculum through Year 6 and does not require special outside knowledge.
